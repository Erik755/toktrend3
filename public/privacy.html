TalkTrend Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TalkTrend. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our service.

2. Data We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to TalkTrend, we collect basic profile information (such as your username and display name) and we request permission to upload videos to your account on your behalf. We do not collect passwords or sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely for the purpose of providing the TalkTrend service: specifically, to authenticate your account and to allow the AI agent to publish generated videos directly to your TikTok profile.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your data is only shared with TikTok via their official API to perform the requested actions.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time from within the TalkTrend dashboard. Upon disconnection, we will delete your access tokens from our servers.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@toktrend3.onrender.com.
